STANDARDS, HEALTH & RECORDS

Verify every claim.

Bellissimo Geni keeps unverified health claims off public profiles. Ask for the records that apply to the specific dog, pairing or puppy you are considering.

01 • STRUCTURE

Breed type and function

Evaluate balance, movement, soundness and overall Cane Corso type together rather than judging a dog from a single photograph or feature.

02 • TEMPERAMENT

Stable behavior

Discuss the individual dog's temperament, handling, training and environment with the kennel so expectations match the dog and the prospective home.

03 • HEALTH RECORDS

Ask for documentation

Where health screening or veterinary records are available, review the actual documents and confirm which dog they belong to before relying on a result.

04 • PUPPY CARE

Records at handover

Before taking a puppy home, confirm the veterinary, vaccination, parasite-control and identification records that will accompany that individual puppy.

05 • BEFORE YOU RESERVE

Confirm the specifics

Availability, parentage, health documentation, care history, reservation terms and handover arrangements should all be confirmed for the exact puppy or pairing you are considering.
