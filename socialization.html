SOCIALIZATION & TRANSITION

Prepared for home.

Good transitions depend on calm handling, sensible exposure, clear routines and owner preparation. Puppy-specific routines and records are confirmed directly with the kennel.

01 • HANDLING

Comfort with handling

Ask how the puppy has been introduced to routine handling such as ears, paws, mouth, grooming and calm restraint, and continue that work gradually at home.

02 • ENVIRONMENT

Everyday exposure

Discuss the sights, sounds, surfaces and household routines the puppy already knows so new experiences can be introduced at an appropriate pace.

03 • ROUTINE

Settling in

Prepare a predictable sleeping, feeding, toilet and quiet-time routine before the puppy arrives. Consistency helps the transition into a new home.

04 • TRAINING

Early guidance

Start with calm boundaries, reward-based learning and age-appropriate expectations. Confirm the puppy's existing cues and routine with the kennel before changing them.

05 • TRANSITION SUPPORT

Confirm the puppy-specific plan

Before collection or delivery, confirm feeding information, veterinary records supplied with the puppy, travel arrangements and any transition notes that apply to that individual puppy.
