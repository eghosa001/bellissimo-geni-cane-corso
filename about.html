ABOUT

Est. 2023.

A pedigree-first programme connecting Italian Cane Corso heritage with Nigerian homes, built on transparent lineage and responsible ownership.

01 • HERITAGE

Italian Cane Corso roots

Bellissimo Geni presents its Cane Corso family with documented lineage where records have been supplied, keeping unverified details off the public profile.

02 • LOCATION

Nigeria-based

Bellissimo Geni is based in Nigeria. Visit and direct contact details are shared through the kennel enquiry process.

03 • MISSION

Responsible ownership

Every litter is placed with owners who understand the guardian breed. Prospective owners receive honest guidance on space, exercise, training, and long-term commitment.

OUR COMMITMENT

What we stand for

Health transparency, documented lineage, responsible placement and clear owner guidance. Public profiles include supplied records without filling unknown facts with guesses.
