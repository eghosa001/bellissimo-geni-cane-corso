BREEDING PROGRAMME

Bred with purpose.

Structure, temperament, health transparency and lifetime guidance. Detailed health claims below are published only after the kennel verifies them.

Verification note: replace this page's general guidance with the kennel's approved health-testing, vaccination, deworming and socialization protocol before launch.
01 • PHILOSOPHY

Purposeful pairings

Pairings are chosen for sound structure, stable temperament and pedigree compatibility — never for volume. Each litter has a recorded sire, dam and rationale the kennel can explain.

02 • STANDARD

The complete Corso

Balanced conformation, functional movement, confidence and composure. Prospective owners receive honest guidance on exercise, training, space and the responsibility of owning a guardian breed.

03 • HEALTH

Transparency first

The kennel publishes only substantiated records: veterinary checks, vaccination / deworming history, and any parent health tests. Ask for documents before reserving; nothing is hidden.

04 • SOCIALIZATION

Raised for home life

Early handling, household sounds, crate introduction and transition guidance. Every new owner leaves with feeding, training and care notes plus ongoing support.
